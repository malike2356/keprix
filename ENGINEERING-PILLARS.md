# Keprix Engineering Pillars

These pillars govern every Keprix prompt, implementation decision, operator workflow, app template, and generated tool.

## 1. Security First

Security is not a feature. It is the foundation every decision sits on.

Every design must start with data protection, access control, auditability, least privilege, secure defaults, safe failure behavior, and recovery.

## 2. AI Powered From Foundation

AI is woven into the architecture from inception, not stitched on after the fact.

The platform must use AI as a native operating layer for setup, guidance, reasoning, workflow execution, localization, self-configuration, and safe automation.

## 3. No Emojis, No Fluff, Direct But Warm

Speak like a human. Clear, concise, professional. No filler.

All operator-facing copy, generated output, docs, prompts, UI text, reports, and agent replies must be useful, plain, and respectful.

## 4. No Em Dashes, No En Dashes, No AI Typography

If a human would not naturally type it, the output should not use it. Use plain ASCII and natural cadence.

Do not use em dashes, en dashes, decorative punctuation, artificial typography, or machine-like phrasing in prompts, docs, generated reports, UI text, or agent messages.

## 5. Cause No Risk To Data, Information, Assets, Or Finance

What people entrust to Keprix must never be harmed.

Every workflow must avoid data loss, financial damage, corrupted assets, credential exposure, unauthorized changes, or irreversible actions without clear permission and confirmation.

## 6. Ethical, Within The Bounds Of Human Ethics

Build in a way that is natural, sustainable, and protective of well-being. Technology serves people, not the other way around.

Keprix must avoid manipulative design, unsafe automation, exploitative workflows, and irresponsible use of AI. It should improve human capability while keeping people in control.

## 7. Compliance With Law And Regulation

Know the jurisdiction. Follow the law.

Keprix and apps built on it must operate within the legal framework of the geography they serve, including data protection, payment, AI, consumer protection, cybersecurity, financial, health, employment, and communications rules where relevant.

## Engineering Requirements

- Every prompt must preserve these pillars.
- Every risky operation must include permission checks, audit logs, and confirmation gates.
- Every generated tool must inherit security, ethics, compliance, and data protection rules.
- Every app template must include privacy, billing, analytics, and user trust safeguards.
- Every user-facing surface must use plain language with no emojis, no fluff, and no forbidden typography.
- Every integration must protect credentials through the vault and redaction layers.
- Every jurisdiction-specific workflow must declare legal and compliance assumptions.
