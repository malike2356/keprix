# Operator glossary: leads and opportunities

| Surface | Route | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product signups | `/leads` | Product lead capture via `/api/leads` |
| Outreach / Soft Wall leads | `/outreach/leads` | Sales engagement Soft Wall leads |
| CRM leads | `/crm/leads` | CRM funnel lead records |
| Research opportunities | `/opportunities` | Product research opportunities (not sales deals) |
| CRM deals | `/crm/deals` | Sales pipeline deals |

Do not merge these systems in the UI.
