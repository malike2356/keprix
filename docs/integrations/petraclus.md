# Petraclus integration

[Petraclus](https://petraclus.com) is a separate offensive-security product. It is not bundled with Keprix Community Edition.

## Boundary

Keprix CE includes passive OSINT and defensive tooling. Active exploitation and vuln scanning belong in Petraclus or Aiva enterprise tiers.

## Connection pattern

Export findings from Keprix research workflows and ingest them in Petraclus according to your org policy. No shared license keys between products.

See [Product boundaries](../product-boundaries/petraclus.md).
