# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, harassment-free experience for
everyone participating in the Keprix project, regardless of background or
experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Assume good intent; ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Credit others for their contributions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or pull
requests that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in a
temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns to **security@carinaai.uk**. Security vulnerability
reports must follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/malike2356/keprix/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
instead of public issues.
