# Responsible Disclosure (keprix)

Report security issues in the **keprix** open-source project:

1. Do not open public GitHub issues for exploitable vulnerabilities.
2. Email security@verlox.uk with reproduction steps and impact.
3. Allow 90 days for a fix before public disclosure.

keprix is self-hosted; many findings may be deployment-specific. Include your keprix
version and configuration (redact secrets).

**Out of scope for this policy:** Petraclus (`keys.petraclus.uk`), Carina Aiva, and
Labyrinth Scout have separate disclosure paths on their own sites.
