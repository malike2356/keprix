Laterite is a weathered surface layer, usually reddish brown, ferruginous, and associated with low yield risk.

Saprolite or weathered granite is partially weathered basement material with moderate permeability.

Fresh basement such as granite, gneiss, schist, or quartzite has low primary porosity. Fracture zones are critical for useful water yield.

Shale and mudstone are usually confining layers and poor aquifers.

Sandstone, sand, and gravel are sedimentary aquifers with moderate to high yield potential.

Alluvium is unconsolidated sediment near rivers and flood plains. It can provide high yield when saturated.
