## Standing instructions for agents

- Read this file before writing into the vault.
- Put unprocessed captures in `00-inbox/`.
- Put project-specific notes in `01-projects/`.
- Put ongoing responsibilities in `02-areas/`.
- Put durable references in `03-resources/`.
- Move stale or completed material to `04-archive/`.
- Prefer wikilinks for related notes and include source links when importing research.

## Inbox processing rules

- Convert raw captures into project, area, or resource notes.
- Keep each note focused on one decision, artifact, or reference.
- Preserve original source URLs and Keprix session ids.

## Keprix session exports

Export session summaries or notebook reports into `00-inbox/` first. After review, link them from the relevant project or resource note.
