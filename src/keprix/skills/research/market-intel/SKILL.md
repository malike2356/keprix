# Market Intelligence

Monitor configured sources and detect market signals.

## Signal Types

- growth, decline, disruption, regulation, stable

## Usage

Configure RSS, newsletter, or competitor site sources. Run monitoring cycles on a schedule or on demand.

Output includes competitor mentions, trend classification, and confidence per signal.
