# Source Credibility

Evaluate sources on four dimensions (0-100 total):

- **Authority** (0-30): primary publishers, government, academic
- **Recency** (0-20): publication date relevance
- **Bias** (0-20): neutral vs opinion-heavy framing
- **Corroboration** (0-30): independent source overlap

Ratings: High (>=70), Medium (45-69), Low (<45).

Low-credibility sources cannot alone support factual claims.
