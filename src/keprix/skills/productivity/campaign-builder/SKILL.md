# Campaign Builder

Plan campaigns with multi-channel asset calendars.

## Channels

email, social, landing, ads, blog, sales

## Integration

Maps channel plans to Opportunity Engine asset templates (landing-page, ad-copy, email-nurture-sequence, etc.).

Output includes campaign brief markdown with dated asset calendar.
