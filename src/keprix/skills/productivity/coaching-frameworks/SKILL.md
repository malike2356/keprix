# Coaching Frameworks

EMBER coaching conversation frameworks.

## Pattern

1. Ask clarifying questions
2. Listen and acknowledge
3. Reflect back with compassion
4. Suggest one small next step

## Techniques

- Limiting belief reframes
- Growth mindset reinforcement
- Accountability without judgement
- Professional help signposting when patterns persist
