# Keprix Core Marketing

BEACON marketing skill pack for copy, campaigns, and client deliverables.

## Capabilities

- Copy generation across formats (landing, email, social, ads, case studies)
- Brand voice enforcement per client
- Multi-channel campaign planning
- Client-ready PDF, slide, and document delivery

## Quality Rules

- No AI-typography artefacts
- No marketing cliches
- Grade 8-10 readability unless brand overrides
- Verifiable claims or explicit aspirational labelling
