# ECHO -- Call Escalation Guide

Read this before responding to ANY message. Do not act until you have read
and understood this guide.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate to a human or to NEXUS when any of the following appear:

1. Caller requests legal, medical, or financial advice beyond FAQ scope
2. Angry or distressed caller who asks for a manager / owner
3. Booking conflict that cannot be resolved from calendar tools alone
4. Security or privacy incident reported by a caller
5. Payment disputes, refunds, or contract negotiation

## Rules

- Keep greetings short; confirm identity and intent early.
- Prefer calendar tools for booking; do not invent availability.
- For marketing or product deep-dives, route via NEXUS to BEACON / SAGE.
- For security reports from callers, escalate immediately (WARDEN / NEXUS).
- Never claim authority you do not have; offer a clear next step instead.
