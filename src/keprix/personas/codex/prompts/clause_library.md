# Clause Library

Jurisdiction-tagged clause types and typical risk profiles for CODEX reviews.

## Limitation of Liability

- **Plain meaning:** Caps how much one party can recover if something goes wrong.
- **Typical risk:** High when liability is uncapped or carved out only for minor breaches.
- **UK note:** B2B caps are often negotiated; consumer contracts have stricter fairness rules.

## Indemnity

- **Plain meaning:** One party agrees to cover the other's losses from specified events.
- **Typical risk:** High when indemnity is one-sided or covers third-party claims broadly.

## Termination

- **Plain meaning:** How either party can end the agreement and what happens on exit.
- **Typical risk:** Medium when termination for convenience is one-sided or notice periods are very short.

## Governing Law and Jurisdiction

- **Plain meaning:** Which country's laws apply and where disputes are heard.
- **Typical risk:** Medium when jurisdiction is unfamiliar or exclusive courts are far from your operations.

## Data Protection

- **Plain meaning:** How personal data is handled, shared, and secured.
- **Typical risk:** High when processor obligations, breach notice, or subprocessor controls are missing (UK GDPR).

## Intellectual Property

- **Plain meaning:** Who owns work product, licences, and background IP.
- **Typical risk:** Medium to High when IP assignment is broad or ownership of deliverables is unclear.

## Non-Compete / Non-Solicit

- **Plain meaning:** Restrictions on working with competitors or poaching staff/customers.
- **Typical risk:** High when scope, duration, or geography is excessive; enforceability varies by jurisdiction.

## Auto-Renewal

- **Plain meaning:** Contract renews unless cancelled by a deadline.
- **Typical risk:** Medium when renewal notice windows are short or fees can increase silently.

## Assignment

- **Plain meaning:** Whether the contract can be transferred to another entity.
- **Typical risk:** Low to Medium when counterparty can assign freely without consent.

## Confidentiality

- **Plain meaning:** What information must be kept secret and for how long.
- **Typical risk:** Low when mutual; Medium when obligations are one-sided or survival period is unclear.
