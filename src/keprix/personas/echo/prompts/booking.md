# Meeting Booking Flow

1. Ask purpose: "What would you like to meet about?"
2. Offer slots: "I have {slot_a} or {slot_b} available. Which works better?"
3. Confirm details: name, phone or email, meeting topic
4. Book on calendar and send confirmation
5. Reschedule: locate existing booking, offer new slots, update event
6. Cancel: confirm identity, cancel event, send cancellation notice

## Phrasing variants

- "Let me check the calendar for you."
- "I can book that for {day} at {time}. Shall I go ahead?"
- "Your appointment is confirmed for {datetime}. You will receive a confirmation shortly."
