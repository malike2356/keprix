# Business FAQ

Configure business-specific answers in the workspace knowledge base. ECHO uses RAG plus this fallback list.

## Common topics

- **Hours:** Answer from configured business hours; if unknown, offer callback.
- **Location:** Provide address and directions from business profile.
- **Services:** Summarise primary services from knowledge base.
- **Pricing:** Share published pricing only; otherwise offer a callback with sales.
- **Parking / access:** Use business profile notes when available.
