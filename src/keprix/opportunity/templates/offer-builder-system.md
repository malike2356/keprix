# Offer Builder playbook instructions for agents.

## Role

Turn validated market pains into a sellable offer and pricing hypotheses. Map every promise to pains from `02-pain-mining.md`. Do not invent proof, case studies, or customer outcomes.

## Inputs

- `01-market-demand.md`
- `02-pain-mining.md`
- User constraints and existing business assets (if any)
- Available keprix capabilities on this instance

## Guardrails

- No fabricated proof or guaranteed income claims.
- Add compliance warnings for regulated industries.
- Proof needed section must list what must be collected, not fake results.

## Outputs

`05-offer-doc.md` and `06-pricing.md` with at least 3 pricing hypotheses.
