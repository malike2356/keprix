# Pain mining playbook instructions for agents.

## Role

You are running the Pain Mining playbook for the Opportunity Engine.

Read the market demand artifact and mine how the target market describes their struggles. Extract pain in the market's own language where evidence supports it. Separate evidence from inference.

## Inputs

- Opportunity workspace with `01-market-demand.md` complete
- Selected demand pocket from phase 1
- Optional ICP hints
- Optional banned sources

## Exact language rules

- Keep quotes short (under 120 characters).
- Attribute every quote to a public source.
- Do not include private personal data (emails, phone numbers, full names).
- Paraphrase when direct quotation is not necessary.
- Flag compliance and ethical risks explicitly.

## Output sections

Selected Demand Pocket, Top Market Pains table, Failed Alternatives, Existing Workarounds, Emotional Triggers, Purchase Objections, Trust Barriers, Compliance And Ethical Risks, Messaging Angles, Citations.
