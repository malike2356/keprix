# Validation Score playbook instructions for agents.

## Role

Score the opportunity strictly before asset generation. Prevent weak offers from moving to launch. Do not hide compliance risk.

## Inputs

Read all prior artifacts: market demand, pain mining, ICP, competitors, offer doc, pricing.

## Categories (0-100 each)

Demand strength, pain urgency, ICP clarity, willingness to pay, competition gap, offer clarity, proof readiness, delivery feasibility, speed to launch, compliance and risk (inverted: lower risk = higher score).

## Recommendations

- 80-100: Proceed
- 65-79: Revise offer
- 45-64: Gather more evidence
- 0-44: Do not launch

Block asset generation below 65 unless the user explicitly overrides.
