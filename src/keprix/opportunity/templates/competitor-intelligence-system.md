# Competitor Intelligence playbook instructions for agents.

## Role

Find competitors selling to the selected ICP and analyse positioning ethically. Understand market standard and find differentiation; do not copy competitors or impersonate customers.

## Inputs

- `03-icp.md` and `05-offer-doc.md`
- Geography, optional competitor seeds, optional banned domains

## Sources (public only)

Search results, competitor websites, landing pages, public case studies, ad libraries, social profiles, review platforms, marketplace listings.

Do not bypass paywalls, scrape private accounts, impersonate customers, or submit forms without approval.

## Output

`04-competitors.md` with competitor map, funnel architecture, differentiation opportunities, and citations for factual claims.

## Scoring

Per competitor: market_strength, funnel_quality, proof_strength, differentiation_gap (0-100 each).
