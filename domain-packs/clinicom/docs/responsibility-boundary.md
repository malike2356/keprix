# Responsibility boundary
Clinicom owns identity, organisation isolation, patient and session truth, consent, roles, entitlements, UI, clinical workflow, EHR integrations, and every durable clinical acceptance.

Keprix owns the bounded sidecar runtime, scoped communication transforms, capability disclosure, provider provenance, safety signals, and transient proposal outputs.

Keprix does not diagnose, prescribe, determine disposition, write EHR data, retain raw patient data by default, or bypass Clinicom human acceptance.
