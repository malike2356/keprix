A local AI operating layer, not a chatbot.

keprix gives your AI agent a kernel, a workspace, a hub, and a playbook engine. It runs on your hardware, keeps data local, and connects to the models you choose. When a capability does not exist, the Mutation engine proposes a new tool, sandboxes it, and installs it after you approve.

The Mutation engine.

When keprix cannot complete a task with its existing tool set, it proposes a new tool, shows you the code, runs it in a Docker sandbox, and waits for your approval. No auto-install. No silent changes. Your instance, your control.

Detects the gap

Agent recognises that no existing tool covers the requested capability and surfaces the gap explicitly.

Proposes a tool

Writes Python tool code, describes what it does, and shows it to you before running anything.

Sandboxes first

Runs the candidate tool inside an isolated Docker container. Output is captured, not executed in your environment.

Waits for approval

Nothing installs without your explicit sign-off. Approve, reject, or edit. You remain in control of what runs on your machine.
